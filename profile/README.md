## English

OP3C guides your organization to **[Open Integration Engine](https://github.com/OpenIntegrationEngine/engine)**,
then runs the engine with you: a proven method, proactive support, and tools built for your team.
Healthcare first, and anywhere data has to move.

Open Integration Engine (OIE) is the community continuation of Mirth Connect, hosted by the Eclipse
Foundation: open source, no licence fees, and governance that belongs to no vendor. OP3C contributes to
the project. Still on Mirth Connect or BridgeLink? [Let's talk anyway](https://op3c.com/en/expertise/engines/):
we can support you until the moment is right to change engines.

### Four areas of expertise, one trade

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔌 **[Integration engines](https://op3c.com/en/expertise/engines/)** | We guide you to OIE and run the engine day to day: upgrades, performance, security, and someone who answers when it matters. |
| 🧩 **[Extensions and tools](https://op3c.com/en/expertise/interfaces/)** | Extensions that add what the engine lacks, and tools around it to design, check and document your interfaces faster. |
| 📊 **[Data analytics](https://op3c.com/en/expertise/data-analytics/)** | Your data flows already hold answers. We help you bring them out, with your teams and at your pace. |
| 🔔 **[Monitoring and alerts](https://op3c.com/en/monitoring/)** | We watch the engine, the servers and the databases. Every alert reaches a named person, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. |

### Extensions for Open Integration Engine

What the engine does not do on its own, we add as extensions, with no change of tool:

- **Security**: protect what flows and who reaches it (sensitive data, accounts, roles).
- **Continuity**: back up, archive and restore the engine and its configurations, at any time.
- **Visibility**: measure what happens inside the engine, channel by channel, where your teams already look.
- **Connectivity**: talk to systems that don't use HL7 (APIs, databases, reference data, lookup tables).

**Already a client?** Report a bug or request a feature in
[op3c-iex-suite-hub](https://github.com/OP3C/op3c-iex-suite-hub). Issues there are public: never include
patient data, credentials or licence keys. Report security vulnerabilities privately, as described in the
[security policy](https://github.com/OP3C/op3c-iex-suite-hub/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

### Extensions pour Open Integration Engine

Ce que le moteur ne fait pas seul, nous l'ajoutons par extensions, sans changer d'outil :

- **Sécurité** : protéger ce qui circule et qui y accède (données sensibles, comptes, rôles).
- **Continuité** : sauvegarder, archiver et restaurer le moteur et ses configurations, à tout moment.
- **Visibilité** : mesurer ce qui se passe dans le moteur, canal par canal, là où vos équipes regardent déjà.
- **Connectivité** : communiquer avec les systèmes qui n'utilisent pas HL7 (API, bases de données, référentiels, tables de correspondance).

**Déjà client?** Signalez un bogue ou proposez une fonctionnalité dans
[op3c-iex-suite-hub](https://github.com/OP3C/op3c-iex-suite-hub). Les billets y sont publics : n'y mettez
jamais de données de patients, d'identifiants ni de clés de licence. Signalez toute vulnérabilité en privé,
selon la [politique de sécurité](https://github.com/OP3C/op3c-iex-suite-hub/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
